# Ground rules: Steady Money

This is a simulated client. The people are played by an AI, and they answer the way real people do: partly, from their own point of view, and sometimes wrongly.

**How the interviews work.** You start with one group call with Dana, Maya and Ray. Then you get a short one-on-one with each of them. Use your time to decide what to build. Ask for the records you need, by name. Nobody sends you everything at once.

**What people say can differ.** If two people disagree, that's useful. Find out why.

**Your time.** You have 20 build hours this week. That includes your interviews.

**The small sample.** Your Discovery materials include a small set of files from a second agency, Bayfield. File names, column headings and the layout of the notes all differ between agencies, so your work can't depend on one layout. Your tool has to take any agency's folder.

**Data.** Everything you receive is for this practice only. Don't post it anywhere public.

**Thursday morning.** We'll post how Friday's run gets scored to your log.

**Friday.** You'll post a 5-minute video with your hand-in. Then on your call, we'll send you a new agency's files, and you run your own written steps on them with no hand edits. The first run counts.
