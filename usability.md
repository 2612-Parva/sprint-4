# Usability — 4 hours

**Finding: Maya will use a Friday list only if it is paper, short, and safe. The director's page is a different reader and must not carry names. One screen that serves both, or a client login, fails her limits.**

Maya described the tool she would keep. It has to tell her who to call on Friday, print on paper, take no more than about ten minutes per counsellor a week, and never ask a client to log in. The reason has to travel with the name, because the person covering the desk when she is away does not share her memory. If it tells her to call someone who asked not to be contacted, she stops using it that day.

She has one hour on Friday morning, about six calls. Today she decides from memory, then checks her notes against the day-one column, because that is where the do-not-contact requests and the newer numbers live.

## Two readers, two outputs

The Friday screen is built as two readings of the same run.

**Maya and Joan, the counsellors.** The client list is one row per person from the attendance sheet: client id, on track or stalled, call or text or paper or do not contact, and a few words of why. The list can be printed. Nothing is sent. Nobody is texted or called by the tool. A do-not-contact row is labelled do not contact, so the person covering the desk can see the ban without remembering it.

**The director, then the funder.** The agency page has the agency name, how many started, how many completed both Money Checks, how many improved, the definition of improved, and the line that this is a halfway view. No client name appears there. Maya said a small subgroup in a close-knit area could pick people out of a named table.

Run Checks stays on the screen. It is the list of notes the tool could not attach safely. It is not a third file. An unclear name, including two people who share a first name or a last name, is a warning. It does not change the other clients.

## What was checked against her limits

- The page opens in a browser on the same computer, at http://127.0.0.1:5000. There is no client account.
- Upload is three files: attendance, diaries, and notes. The user runs the written steps. The first run counts.
- On a successful run the agency name and the client count are the first things on the page, then the summary, then the client list, then Run Checks.
- On a notes failure the page shows only `ERROR: Cannot read notes. Halting.` There is no download.
- Cole Street's summary contains the counts and the halfway sentence, and it does not contain client names. The same is true of Bayfield.

## Who was not in the room

Joan is not on the call, and half the clients are hers. Maya marked Joan's 24 from Joan's notes and a talk with her. The screen can show Joan's clients, but Joan has not said whether ten minutes and a paper list fit her Friday.

Dana does not use the call list. She needs the one page. Tess trains the Money Check and was named as the person who would know the score wording. She has not reviewed the screen.

The cover person at the desk is the reason the "why" column exists. That person was not interviewed. The test of the column is Maya's own statement that the reason has to travel with the name.

## What would make her stop

Maya named one immediate stop: a contact suggestion for someone who asked not to be contacted. The list is built so a matched ban becomes do not contact. A partial limit, such as "no texts" alone, does not become a full ban, and it does not become a suggestion to text.

The other stop is extra work that only appears at renewal. The same run produces the Friday list and the director page, so the renewal page is not a second project in February.
