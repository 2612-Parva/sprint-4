# Value — 6 hours

**Finding: the page a director can sign this week is a halfway Money Check view, not a diary-reminder result. Session 6 has not happened, the diary pages never reach Dana, and the renewal question is whether the numbers can be traced to the agency's own records.**

Dana spent most of last week on the phone with directors about January renewals. A couple were direct that they want outcome numbers this year. The Steady Money agreement is two pages on fees and materials. Dana said there is no report template, "that's the whole problem, nobody's ever produced outcome numbers for a funder."

The January list has twelve agencies, each at a $4,800 annual fee, renewing between 8 January and 30 January 2027. Five require proof because a funder asked in writing: Cole Street, Bayfield, Northgate Neighbours, Three Bridges Community Hub, and Mill Lane Family Services. The board date in the materials is Thursday 3 December 2026. Cole Street is one of the five, and Maya said she would recommend renewal if she can hand her director one honest page.

## What the records can show now

Maya's funder page needs three counts, with the rule written plainly: how many started, how many stuck with it, and how many are better off. She said she would hesitate if the numbers could not be traced back to Cole Street's own records, or if the work only showed up at renewal time.

The closest measure of better off is the Money Check: five questions at sessions 1, 4, and 6, each scored 1 to 5, total 25. Maya types the scores into the attendance sheet and has never added them up. Session 6 is 17 November 2026. The files stop at session 4. By 3 December the session 6 scores can exist, but they are not in this week's files, so a final "better off" number cannot be claimed now.

The page this week uses these proposed definitions. They are proposed, not agreed. Dana wants to see how the scores move before she decides, and Maya wants whoever sets the rule to say it plainly.

- **Started:** everyone on the attendance sheet.
- **Completed both Money Checks:** both scores are present. A blank or n/a is missing, not zero.
- **Improved:** both scores are present and the second is higher than the first. An equal or lower score is not improved.
- **Halfway:** the note on the page says final scores are not yet available.

On the sample runs the page reads:

- Cole Street: 48 started, 42 completed both Money Checks, 36 improved.
- Bayfield: 6 started, 5 completed both, 2 improved.

No client name is on that page. Maya said the area is close-knit and a small subgroup could pick people out.

## Why the reminder app is not this week's evidence

Dana opened from a reminder-app idea. The call does not support treating diary reminders as the renewal proof.

- Ray said nobody chases him when a diary week is missed.
- Maya said the diary page goes in the client's paper folder and stays there. Nothing goes to Dana. Those records are Cole Street's.
- Dana said she has never seen a client's diary page since she left the counselling room.
- A diary that was not set, was put on hold, or was missed because the session ran long is not the same as a client who drifted off. Maya said to check those against the notes.
- Ray could not point to one diary week that left him financially better off in a way the Money Check would capture cleanly. Dana also said the five questions can miss a change that matters to the client.

So this week did not claim that diary completion causes Money Check improvement. The honest finding is the halfway count, labelled as halfway.

## What the tool also gives Maya on Friday

Maya said a once-a-year report dies by February. She will keep it up if it also tells her who to call on Friday. She has one hour on Friday morning, about six calls, and she currently goes by memory. She already keeps a marked-up list of who is on track or stalled and how she would reach them. Newer notes beat the day-one contact column.

The second output is that list: one row per client, with status, way to reach, and a few words of why. A client who asked not to be contacted is never suggested. Maya said if the tool ever tells her to call someone who asked not to be contacted, she stops using it that day.
